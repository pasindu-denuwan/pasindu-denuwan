## About Me

I'm a Computing undergraduate at the Faculty of Computing, Sabaragamuwa University of Sri Lanka, passionate about connecting design, business needs, project execution, and software quality.

I enjoy understanding user problems, designing intuitive interfaces, coordinating teams, analyzing requirements, and helping deliver reliable digital solutions.

My approach combines creative thinking, structured planning, analytical problem-solving, and attention to detail.

- **Project Management** — Planning, coordination, teamwork, and delivery.
- **Business Analysis** — Requirements gathering, process improvement, and translating business needs into solutions.
- **Quality Assurance** — Test planning, functional testing, defect identification, and quality improvement.
